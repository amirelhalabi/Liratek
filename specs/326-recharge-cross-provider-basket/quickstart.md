# Quickstart: LIRA-326

1. Mobile Recharge → Katsh: pick 2 cards. → Whish App → Bills: pick 1 item. → back to Katsh: the 2 cards are still picked (tab badge 2).
2. Proceed to Pay (any tab): the window lists Katsh and Whish App groups with subtotals; pay cash.
3. Transactions: one Katsh sale and one Whish App sale; Katsh balance −(Katsh costs), Whish App balance −(Whish cost); General +total.
4. Sessions: a closed "Mobile Recharge basket" session links both sales.
5. Refund the basket from either row → every balance back to its starting value. Refunding one sale alone is refused.
6. Pick 1 iPick + 1 Katsh, pay from the Katsh tab → iPick balance −iPick cost, Katsh balance −Katsh cost.
7. With a customer session open, pick on iPick and Katsh → the session basket gets one entry per provider.
