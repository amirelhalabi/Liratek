## How to run e2e (required sequence)

1. `yarn dev` and wait for it to finish starting (rebuilds `better-sqlite3` to the Electron ABI + builds `electron-app/dist`).
2. **Stop** `yarn dev` (frees port 5173 + the Electron instance).
3. `yarn test:e2e`.

After editing `packages/core/**`: `cd packages/core && npm run build` then sync `dist` into
`node_modules/@liratek/core/dist` (Windows: `xcopy /e /y /q`; macOS: `rsync -a --delete packages/core/dist/ node_modules/@liratek/core/dist/`), or the runtime runs stale code.

## E2E assertion discipline (shared accumulating DB — CLAUDE.md rule 15)

- Match the row by **identity** (type + provider/`service_type`, `source_id`, `item_key`, or a unique amount/label) — never `getRecent(...)[0]` or `tbody tr.first()`.
- Assert **deltas**: snapshot the drawer/ledger/balance immediately before the action, compare after. Never absolute totals or row position.
- One action can write **multiple** unified-transaction rows (e.g. a `FINANCIAL_SERVICE` + an auto `SUPPLIER_PAYMENT` sibling); `created_at` is second-granular so same-second rows tie.
- IPC-driven specs (`appPage.evaluate(() => window.api.*)`) are preferred for money-flow correctness; DOM specs for UI-visibility items.

### B6b — Loto supplier ledger uses an inverted sign convention (found during C5 e2e)
`LotoTicketRepository.createTicket` books the shop's liability as a NEGATIVE `PAYMENT` entry (−(sale−commission)); every other supplier books debt as POSITIVE. The Suppliers page sums ledger rows, so LOTO reads inverted vs all other suppliers. Loto settlement flows are built around this convention.
**Blocked on: scheduling — a coordinated redesign** (repo + settlement + Suppliers display + migration for historical rows), not a drive-by fix.

### Profit refund attribution (per-cashier) — policy, from the profit-audit review
Refunding a fully-paid sale leaves the ORIGINAL sale's profit credited to the seller (the SALE txn stays ACTIVE) while the negated REFUND is debited to the **refunder** (whoever clicked refund). Aggregate totals are correct (net 0); only the **by-cashier** split is affected — the seller keeps credit and the refunder shows a loss for admin work. Void behaves differently (voids the original → both zero).
**Blocked on: product decision** — should a refund's profit reversal land on the original seller, the refunder, or zero both (void-style)? Currently no data is corrupted, so left as-is. (Note: `getByClient` is unaffected — the refund copies the original client_id.)

### Profit daily-chart refund dating — pre-existing accrual inconsistency, from the review
`getByDate` drops a refunded sale's revenue/cost retroactively (current-state `is_refunded`) but nets its profit at the REFUND's date (accrual). A report window covering the sale date but NOT the refund date shows the sale's +profit with $0 revenue/cost. `getSummary.getSalesProfit` dates the refund by the sale's date (consistent); `getByDate` diverges. Pre-existing (from the transaction-based profit refactor), not introduced by the audit fixes.
**Blocked on: a decision** — make `daily_sales_profit` net the refund at the sale's date (retroactive, matches revenue/cost) vs. keep accrual. Low $ impact (only cross-window sale refunds).
