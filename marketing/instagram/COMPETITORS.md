# Competitors — research notes (2026-10-17)

Read-only web research. Prices and quotes are as published on each site on this date; re-check before quoting them anywhere.

## Direct: Table POS — tablelb.com
- The closest competitor. Its phone-shop page (tablelb.com/phone-shops) is headed "Which handset, sold to whom, and when?"
- Verified on that page (verbatim):
  - Alfa/Touch: "Track your Alfa and Touch lines and balances, and record credit transfers and recharge-card sales with each operator's fees. It's a ledger — it doesn't connect to the operators."
  - Serial + warranty on the sale line; repairs rung as a service and left on the customer's account; debts settled "in either currency".
  - "Quote the handset in dollars and take it in lira, dollars or both."
  - "POS licence $10 / month or $100 / year", "14 days free · no credit card", an owner phone app, support on WhatsApp.
- Not on the phone-shop page: OMT/Whish transfers as a module. Whish/OMT appear only as payment methods (homepage, per research agent — not re-checked).
- No social accounts or testimonials found.

## Weak or indirect
- **Overclock** (overclocklb.com, @over.clocklb):
  - LinkedIn: "Powering mobile shops with smart POS solutions for sales, inventory, and debt management."
  - Website is an IT / CCTV / gadget shop with no POS product page or pricing. LinkedIn size "1 employee".
  - Instagram mixes gaming gear and POS clips.
- **Sandooq** (sandooq.app): offline-first; LBP + USD on one receipt; a drawer per currency. $39/mo Starter, $89/mo Pro. No phone-shop features.
- **MAPOS** (mapos.tech): an ERP across many sectors, USD/LBP, quote after a demo. Instagram @i.madtechnology.
- **Managely** (managely.cloud): ERP with a dual-pricing engine. 7-day trial. YouTube @managelyclouderp.
- **AR POS** (poslebanon.co): a retail/supermarket POS. The site did not render.
- **Mousaheb:** unverified, probably a side project.

## What this means for posts
- **Not differentiators** (everyone claims them): USD+LBP, offline, Arabic, IMEI/warranty, Alfa recharge, debts, owner app.
- **LiraTek angles nobody else markets:**
  - OMT/Whish send and receive with the fee worked out, plus checking against OMT's statement.
  - Counting a drawer any time, with the sales since its last count.
  - Daily closing with profit per part of the shop.
  - Keeping the small change as profit.
  - The owner recording after-hours Whish App / OMT App transfers from the phone.
- **Price anchor:** Table at $10/mo with a 14-day trial. LiraTek posts state no price (the landing page has none). If pricing comes up, expect this comparison.
- **Never write "the only POS that…"** — Table covers much of the phone-shop list. Lead with the specific job instead ("OMT fee worked out"), not exclusivity.

## Feature check: competitor features vs LiraTek code (2026-10-17)

### LiraTek already has
- Rate stored per sale
- Alfa/MTC line balances
- Recharge cards with fees
- Repairs on the customer's account
- IMEI/serial + warranty
- A drawer per currency
- Barcode labels
- Supplier purchases
- Excel export

### Missing or partial — what competitors advertise that LiraTek lacks

| Feature | Who has it | LiraTek status |
|---|---|---|
| Arabic UI in the app | Table, Sandooq, MAPOS, Managely | **Missing**: no i18n, the app is English-only (only the landing page has Arabic) |
| Free trial with self-signup | Table (14 days), Managely (7 days) | Partial: signup exists, no trial or billing |
| Send receipt / quote on WhatsApp | MAPOS, Managely | Partial: WhatsApp API settings exist, nothing sends |
| Price levels (retail / wholesale) | Table (3 levels) | Missing |
| Variants ("128 GB Black") | Table | Partial: `color` only |
| Several tills at once | Table (extra terminal $10) | Missing |
| Offline web app | Sandooq, Table, MAPOS | Partial: desktop is offline by nature; web is not |
| Online store sharing stock | Table ($5/mo) | Missing |
| Quotes / estimates | MAPOS | Missing |
| Loyalty points, payroll, delivery | MAPOS / Managely | Missing (out of scope for phone shops) |
