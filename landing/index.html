LiraTek on your phone

Your shop with you, after closing too.

A phone app for the shop owner. When a customer messages you after hours, record the transfer from home — it goes into your shop's books just like one made at the counter.

  • Record Whish App and OMT App transfers, on the customer's account or paid into your Whish or OMT wallet.
  • See your drawer and wallet balances live, and the sales since the last count on each wallet.
  • See who owes you, and record a repayment into a wallet.
  • Tap Save twice by mistake and the sale is still booked once.
  • Dark, light, or the same as your phone.
