## Hero verdict

Better, ship it. Removing the pill + ping dot, the ghost button and the blue glow left one calm column, one primary action and the video as the focal point. Three loose ends:

1. Two different "primary" actions: header blue button = Create your shop, hero blue button = WhatsApp, and the closing CTA is WhatsApp again. Pick one primary per page, or make the header one secondary.
2. At >=1240px the hero still uses `padding: 96px 0 112px` — that rule sits inside the badge media block (styles.css ~891-894) and was sized for the floating badges. Now it's dead air above and below the hero (visible at 1440). Delete it with the badges, or keep the 56/64 base.
3. Lead repeats the H1 ("Prices, change and drawers are kept in USD and LBP side by side" after "in dollars and lira"). Optional trim.
