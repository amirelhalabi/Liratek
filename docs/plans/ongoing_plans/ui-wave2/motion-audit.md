## C. Rejected

- Count-up / rolling digits on Total Due or line prices (Cart.tsx:125) — shows wrong money while animating; cashier reads it to the customer.
- Any animation on the barcode/search input or results grid re-render while typing (ProductSearch.tsx) — keyboard/scanner, 100+/day.
- Exit animations on cart-line remove, Clear cart, Checkout close/Escape, strip dismissal — the action must feel instant; exits add latency.
- "Fly to cart" item animation from tile to cart — showy, ~600ms, blocks nothing but distracts at speed.
- Stagger on product grid / today's-sales grid (ProductSearch.tsx:417,624) — refetches on every search keystroke; stagger would replay constantly.
- Success celebration (confetti/bounce) on Complete Sale — happens hundreds of times/day; not a rare moment.
- Infinite `animate-pulse` on the newest minimized order (index.tsx:1034) — constant peripheral motion; recommend a static dot instead.
- Installing tw-animate-css to "revive" the dead classes — would turn on 500ms page fades on 20 pages.
- Animating the checkout change/remaining figures in MultiPaymentInput (packages/ui) — money the cashier must read exactly; out of sales scope anyway.
