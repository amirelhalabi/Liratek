## 👥 Staff, users & login
- Staff can now open the Transactions & Audit page. Settings stays admin-only, and Profits stays behind its password.
- Staff can see the Void / Refund buttons; they say an admin is needed.
- Web app: Settings → Users now really works (create, deactivate, change a role, set a password). Before, it showed success but saved nothing.
- Every user action now shows a success or error message, and you can't remove admin from your only admin by mistake.
- Usernames are no longer case-sensitive ("Admin" = "admin"). If two accounts differed only by capitals, one was renamed (e.g. "Admin.dup-2"); where login history shows which account is yours, your original name is restored automatically.
- "Keep me signed in on this device" (was "Remember me for 1 day"): unticked, you are signed out after 8 hours idle instead of 30 minutes; ticked, you stay signed in for a week of use.
- A brief database hiccup no longer signs you out with "session expired".

## 📋 Transactions page
- New "Ret. Credits" column shows the credits returned on MTC/Alfa card sales.
- Top-ups can be voided or refunded from here, and the drawer is reversed.
- The Type filter takes several types at once, and on the web app it no longer mixes in other types.
- A checkpoint's payment detail names each drawer instead of repeating "Checkpoint Adjustment".
- Customer sessions: the payment summary shows once per session, not on every row.
- A session basket can be refunded as a whole, or you can refund just one item. The customer's account is reduced first, and the rest goes back through the refund form. (A basket with a loto prize or cash-out is refunded as a whole.)
- Sorting by Amount now orders LBP transactions correctly alongside USD ones.
- The automatic SMS-fee expense rows are hidden by default (filter by Expense to see them); the Expenses page still lists every expense.
- Bills-only iPick/Katsh settlements get their own amber marker instead of the green "cash in" arrow.

## 🛒 POS & refunds
- "Refund Sale" and "Refund item" in POS open the refund form, where you choose the drawer or method the money goes back through.
- The refund form has a "Returned phones" section: mark a returned phone as defective or give it a new warranty date.
- A phone marked defective shows "— Defective" in POS, and selling it asks "sell anyway?" first.
- Every refund window has an exchange rate you can edit, starting at the rate the sale was paid at, and you can give the money back in any mix of $ and LBP.
- Sales made in a customer session keep the customer's name.
- A parked draft no longer shows as a sale, and saving a draft again no longer adds its payment to the drawer twice.
- Cart lines and the checkout list show a warranty badge (e.g. "12 months").
- With "For Partner" ticked, POS and Loto sales no longer offer "Customer Account" as a payment method.
- Refunding a sale whose items were all already refunded one by one now says so clearly.

## 🎟 Sessions & Loto
- A loto cash prize is subtracted from what the customer buys, so the change you really hand back (e.g. $40 + 10,000 LBP) is recorded correctly.
- The session's account balance updates right away after any account entry, repayment or checkout.
- Web app: Loto checkpoint history shows correctly, "Create Checkpoint" works even before the first checkpoint, and the settlement check no longer always fails.
- Editing a Loto checkpoint's note now saves to that checkpoint.

## 💸 OMT / Whish & suppliers
- OMT receive: the fee only works out your commission. It is never collected, never moves the drawer, and OMT is owed the full amount.
- OMT receives, including Cash to Business, can be saved without an OMT fee.
- A fee you charge on a Whish receive counts as your profit.
- OMT, iPick and the OMT App wallet show as one OMT account on the Suppliers page: one card, one combined history, and one payment settles everything.
- Topping up the OMT App wallet adds to what you owe OMT, instead of taking cash from the OMT drawer. A new "Cash Out to OMT" button sends wallet money back, and the 0.1% commission counts as profit.
- When settling OMT or any supplier you can pay more than what's selected. The extra is kept as a credit the supplier owes you, and you use it at the next settlement.
- A supplier settlement must match what you're settling. It can no longer be marked paid without money actually moving.
- Settings → Service Providers: new "Part of account" option to group a supplier under an account.
- Shops whose main system is Whish now see their OMT account (and the iPick / OMT App debt under it) on the Suppliers page.
- Editing a past OMT/Whish/iPick/Katsh/Binance entry now saves the client name too.
- The Services, Recharge and Profits pages show how many transfers are "awaiting settlement" instead of a bare 0.00 commission.

## 📱 MTC / Alfa
- Selling credits reduces your shop line balance (Settings → Shop Lines and on the MTC/Alfa page).
- A buy-back records exactly the credits bought ($9 = +$9, no more double).
- Typing one of your own shop lines shows a checkbox: ticked = buy the credits back, unticked = charge the customer for using the line. No SMS fee either way.
- You can sell more days than a line holds. The line shows the days sold ahead, the sale reminds you to recharge, and a red countdown stays on the Dashboard until you do. A "days still to send" list with "Mark sent" tracks what you still owe the customer.
- Buying credits from a client: pay them from any drawer, split across drawers, in $, LBP or both. Pick the client from your client list. The fee you keep counts as profit, and paying less than the credits minus your fee is refused.
- The SMS transfer fee on credit sales is recorded as its own expense, so the recharge page shows the full margin (overall net profit is unchanged).
- New installs: the Alfa $77.28 cards correctly default to 73.50 returned credits.

## 📦 Inventory
- You can stock phone numbers (lines) as items and buy them on credit from a dealer through normal stock intake. A number is found however it's typed (spaces, +961), and the same number can't be added twice.
- The product edit form has its own "Adjust Stock" button.
- Receiving stock from a supplier adds to what you owe them at that moment, at the unit cost you enter; selling, refunding or re-pricing no longer changes that balance. An "Old stock" checkbox adds stock without supplier debt.
- Quantity is read-only on the edit form; stock changes go through Adjust Stock, which asks for the unit cost.
- Oldest stock sells first at its own cost, so profit uses what that unit really cost. Adjust Stock shows a "Cost Batches" list, and products with stock at two costs get a "mixed cost" tag.
- Voiding a stock delivery removes it and its debt, but is refused once any unit from it was sold.
- The supplier "Write off" button is gone; use the discount on the Pay form when a supplier forgives part of a debt. The product-supplier history reads "Stock received" / "Payment".
- The batch-edit "Unit" field now saves; Phone Units shows "Product deleted" next to units whose product was deleted.

## 🔁 Debts, Exchange, Hold Money & more
- Debts: paying a debt with mixed $ and LBP while keeping the change now clears it exactly, with no leftover like −340 LBP.
- Exchange: type what the customer gets (e.g. 50 EUR) and the page works out how much they hand over.
- Hold Money: drop-off and pickup use the payment form (any drawer or wallet). A hold can be picked up in parts, with the remaining amount shown, and paid out in another currency at the day's buy rate.
- Custom Services: Syria transfers OUT, under Via Partner → "Pay out". The customer is paid from the General drawer, your commission counts that day, and the partner owes you the payout on the Partners page.
- Maintenance: a repair job can be saved without a phone number.
- Expenses: expenses you add now show on the Profits page and reduce net profit. The Expenses page tells you why a delete was refused.
- Maintenance: you can attach inventory parts to a repair job. Stock is taken when the job is saved, part prices go on the bill and the receipt, and part margins count toward the job's profit. The checkout discount applies to labour only, and changing a job's status no longer erases its discount.
- Exchange: converting into LBP no longer shows a negative dollar amount, and new descriptions show thousands separators (e.g. "$100").
- Paying in another currency: the rate a cashier types may now be up to 15% away from the shop's rate (was 10%).

## 📊 Profits page
- New "Total Net Profit" card: Gross − Expenses = Net, for $ and LBP.
- By Module: tap a row to see the exact transactions behind it (they add up exactly to the row), plus a greyed "not counted yet" list with the reason for each. Every module is covered.
- All tabs (Overview, By Module, By Date, By Cashier, By Client) give the same totals.
- LBP amounts are no longer added up as dollars. The headline reads "Turnover (incl. transfers & exchange)", and debt entries never count as revenue.
- Profit that was missing now counts: kept change, top-ups and buy-backs, discounts, bills commission.
- OMT/Whish kept change and a Whish receive fee count as earned right away.
- New "Waiting for repayment" line on the Financial Services card.
- By Payment is now "Cash intake by method": it leaves out voided sales, shows what really stayed in the drawer after change, and has a column for debt repayments.
- Pending tab: partner sales are no longer counted twice, discounts are applied, and every outstanding sale shows. "Unpaid sales" subtracts refunded items.
- Commissions tab: follows the dates you pick, is behind the password, and shows $ and LBP separately.
- A named walk-in's kept change is listed under their name.
- LBP rows no longer show a 0% margin, a loss is no longer shown in green, and a failed load shows an error instead of "no data".
- Staff can open the Profits page too, but everyone (admins included) types a separate Profits password; it unlocks for 15 minutes and locks when you leave the page. An admin sets it in Settings → Profits Password, and Profits stays locked until it is set.
- When a partner pays back part of what they owe, Profits shows the same share of that job's profit (50% paid = 50% of the profit).

## 🧾 Closing report
- Profit on the closing report matches the Profits page for the day, LBP included. (A $20 buy-back no longer counts as $18 profit.)
- The PDF shows net profit for $ and LBP, the time it was taken ("Profit as of HH:MM"), and "hidden" for staff who haven't unlocked Profits.

## 🏠 Dashboard
- The sales line on the chart is "Product & Telecom Sales" (no voids or payouts), and the profit line matches Profits → By Date, for both $ and LBP.
- The tile is now "Net Profit – Last 30 Days".
- The "awaiting settlement" banner no longer shows "$0.00 owed", and it shows LBP amounts and LBP commissions too.
- The top-up windows' Cancel and Confirm buttons are always reachable on small screens.
- Charts (trend, Today's Sales, Top Debtors) now display on narrow screens too.
- The OMT/Whish App and System drawers no longer disappear when the iPick/Katsh module is switched off.

## ⚙️ Settings
- New Settings → Reset Data (admin): wipes sales, stock, clients, ledgers and history, but keeps users, shop setup, modules, currencies and settings. The desktop app takes a backup first.
- New Settings → Signed-in Devices: see where your account is signed in and sign out one device or all others.
- The app no longer shows "Corner Tech" as a default shop name; the login screen shows LiraTek until you name your shop.
- Settings → Modules & Drawers says which drawers each module owns.

## 🖥 Desktop app
- Backups are one complete file again, and the backup list shows your backups (it was always empty).
- New Settings → Licence (admin): see your plan, status and expiry, with a "Check now" button. The app never locks the shop if it can't reach the server.
- Voice commands: "OMT" or "Whish" opens the OMT/Whish page, and "services" opens Services.

## 🌐 Web app
- Opening a past sale no longer says "Sale not found".
- Cancelling a draft order and deleting an expense work.
- When a sale or an expense is refused (e.g. out of stock), you see the real reason.
- Each shop signs in at its own address (yourshop.liratek.shop), with the shop's name on the login page. Different shops can use the same usernames.
- Changes made on another device (sales, drawers, debts, sessions) show up automatically on the Dashboard, top bar and session list.
- Shop data is backed up off-site continuously.
- A wrong password says so; opening the login page a few times no longer triggers "Too many login attempts"; log out always works, and an ended session returns you to the login screen.
- After an update, an open tab reloads itself instead of failing.
- Screens that used to work only on desktop now work in the browser: sale details (refund, item refund, notes), today's sales in POS search, custom-service search and presets, WhatsApp from the client form, "save as client", supplier and category settings, barcode lookup, the cash report, edit history, and service-debt details.
- Settle Debt payments, Excel import of clients and debts (up to 10 MB), shop logo upload, Settings → Mobile Services and Shop Config all work.

## 🌙 Work done after midnight (00:00 – 03:00)
- Web app: a checkpoint taken after midnight shows under today.
- Web app: the opening-balance check at login, SIM line validity and grace days, and voucher expiry use your shop's own date.
- Web app: the monthly loto fee on the first Monday is no longer skipped.
- Both apps: a warranty that ended yesterday no longer shows "Covered".
- Desktop app: the "today" and "this month" figures on the OMT/Whish and Custom Services cards no longer read zero.

## 🧩 General
- New "What's new" window: after each update it shows what changed, once. Tap the version number at the bottom of the sidebar to see it again.
- Opening a dropdown no longer drags the page sideways, and long lists scroll.
- Vouchers page: the status filter matches the status each voucher shows.
